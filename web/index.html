01

Screen & Keyboard Clean

No more locking your screen, shutting down, or carefully dodging the trackpad. Wipe off dust, oil, and crumbs in seconds while staying in your flow.

02

True Hardware Lock

Unlike apps that just drop clicks, Trackpad Toggle invokes CGAssociateMouseAndMouseCursorPosition(0) to disconnect hardware deltas. The cursor sprite physically cannot move.

03

Kernel-Level Hotkey

Built with Carbon’s RegisterEventHotKey. It requires zero Accessibility permissions and works at the kernel event layer — you can never lock yourself out.

04

Stealth Background Mode

Prefer a zero-clutter menu bar? Toggle off the menu-bar icon in Settings. The app runs completely invisible in the background, waking on your shortcut or Spotlight launch.
