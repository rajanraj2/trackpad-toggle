Wipe Down Freely

Clean dust, crumbs, and smudges without moving your cursor, closing windows, or making rogue clicks.

02

Total Input Freeze

The cursor is locked completely in place. Swipes, taps, physical clicks, and gestures are fully suppressed.

03

Instant Shortcut

Press ⌃⌥⌘T anytime to toggle. Works system-wide across all applications and spaces without delay.

Stealth Mode

Hide the menu bar icon in Settings for zero visual clutter. The app runs completely silently in the background.
